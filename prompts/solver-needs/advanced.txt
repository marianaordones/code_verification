
ADDITIONAL SEARCH EVENTS — OPTIONAL ADVANCED BLOCK
Both experimental arms receive the same instructions, base statistics/instantiations,
and input-quantifier complement. Only one arm may also receive advanced_internals.
There are three TOTAL proposals. Return the same four-field proof-slot JSON action;
these diagnostics do not grant permission to change implementation or contracts.

The advanced block is collected by separate bounded, instrumented Z3 diagnostic
replays of the saved failing queries. It is not the original Verus search and need
not be the same search as solver_internals. queries records outcomes, limits and
availability. Never silently combine events from different queries or replays.

matching contains recorded E-matching events. trigger is the matched pattern;
top_level_matched_terms are the concrete terms named in the match; bindings records
terms in trace order and their paths to equality-class roots. Do not guess a
multi-variable source-variable mapping from this order. Variables such as (:var 0)
are bound SMT variables. These are actual match records, unlike the input term
examples in complementary_internals. instance_count=0 means a match was recorded
without a corresponding observed instance; it is not proof of a discarded formula.

formula_ref points to formulas in this block; a unique qid associates an INPUT
formula with the event, but preprocessing may have transformed the search formula.
An entry in formulas can instead reference an exactly identical formula in
solver_internals (query_id and zero-based group_index) or complementary_internals
(zero-based item_index). Those references are checked by full formula hash, not
by qid similarity. The shared packages are not removed or modified for this.
ambiguous_qid is not an established formula association. query_id scopes every
trace ID: #25 in one query is unrelated to #25 in another. IDs/checksums are labels,
not program facts or source locations. resolved=false is explicitly partial data.

equalities_used contains equality pairs explicitly named by a match. Binding
path_to_root may additionally show equality rewrites. In steps_raw, lit cites a
literal, cg denotes congruence, th denotes a theory justification, and root marks
an equivalence-class root. Complete paths in the audit do not amount to an
independent checked proof; supports are not recursively verified. steps_omitted
and equality_pairs_omitted mark truncation. Never treat a partial path as complete.
Use a relevant equality as a hypothesis for a justified intermediate assertion or
ghost term, and verify that hypothesis from the program's available facts.

chains references matching events or referenced_matches. An edge means a term
was attached to the E-graph during an earlier instance and explicitly named in a
later match. causal_creation_proven=false: this does not prove first creation,
necessity, or a complete causal dependency. A short chain may suggest a missing
intermediate proof step, not a lemma to assume without justification.

non_inst_clauses are callback inferences other than quantifier instantiations.
clause_literals_smt is one DISJUNCTION. For example ["(not P)", "Q"] means
P implies Q, not that P and Q were separately established. A generic smt hint is
not a detailed theory derivation; euf/farkas/bound/implied-eq are retained only if
actually observed. Dependencies and branch validity may be incomplete, and these
clauses are not automatically unconditional lemmas or useful learned conflicts.
Prefer clauses relating the failed goal's operations; encoding housekeeping alone
does not justify nonlinear mode, additional fuel, or a collection law.

Try to identify one concrete missing bridge supported by the program: an equality,
an argument term exposing a useful quantifier instance, an intermediate assertion,
or justified unfolding. These events are evidence for repair hypotheses, not new
axioms. No block, no event, or an omitted event does not establish absence from
the full search. Verus remains the final judge of the proposed repair.
